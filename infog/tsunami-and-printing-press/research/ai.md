# AI as capital sink, bubble odds, and AI deflation vs inflation — 2026-10-14

All figures fetched 2026-10-14 (CEST). FACT = primary/high-quality source fetched; REPORTED = single/second-hand; FORECAST = named institution + date; UNVERIFIED = unconfirmed. Nothing interpolated; gaps stated as gaps.

## headline

- **2026 capex: big four guided to $720–745bn, ~$835bn including Oracle's fiscal-2027 guide, against $379bn actually spent in 2025.** Alphabet lifted to $195–205bn; Oracle's guide rose from ~$70bn to $90–95bn in six weeks ([TMT Finance](https://www.tmtfinance.com/intel/2026-hyperscaler-capex-tops-us700bn-analysis); [CRISIL](https://integraliq.crisil.com/en/homepage/what-we-think/all-our-thinking/blog/2026/10/the-ai-debt-supercycle.html)).
- **The macro payoff is disputed tenfold: BIS ~1pp of 2025 US real GDP growth, JPMorgan 0.47pp of the past year's 2.1%, ING ≈one-third, Goldman ISG ≈0.1pp** ([BIS AER 2026](https://www.bis.org/publ/arpdf/ar2026e.htm) p.9; [JPM AM](https://am.jpmorgan.com/us/en/asset-management/liq/insights/market-insights/market-updates/on-the-minds-of-investors/how-much-of-the-ai-boom-is-adding-to-us-growth/); [ING](https://think.ing.com/opinions/how-much-is-ai-contributing-to-us-economic-growth/); [Goldman PWM](https://pwm.gs.com/apac/en-gb/insights/markets-and-investing/investment-strategy/ai-impact-gdp-productivity-jobs)).
- **Concentration is past 2000: JPMorgan's 41-stock AI basket = 49% of S&P 500 value, top 10 ≈41% (vs ~27% in 2000), Mag7 34.9%, Nvidia ≈8% and $5.5tn** ([Seeking Alpha](https://in.tradingview.com/news/seekingalpha:181036896094b:0-jpmorgan-s-ai-basket-represents-49-of-the-s-p-500/); [AInvest](https://www.ainvest.com/news/ten-stocks-70-index-work-number-timestamp-2610/); [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/research/magnificent-seven-sp-500/)).
- **Financing has already repriced: hyperscaler issuance ~$109bn (2025) → ~$218bn in 2026 YTD, ~18% of US corporate IG supply; Oracle 5y CDS at a record 261bp, market-implied 5-year default probability >20%** ([CRISIL](https://integraliq.crisil.com/en/homepage/what-we-think/all-our-thinking/blog/2026/10/the-ai-debt-supercycle.html); [Bloomberg via Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/ai-debt-spree-hammers-tech-222451927.html)).
- **Both forces are measurable: a fixed AI capability falls ~13×/year in price (725× from o3 to GPT-5.6 Luna in <18 months), while DRAM contract prices rose 58–63% QoQ in Q2 2026, US CPI is 3.35%, euro HICP 3.8% (energy +18.8%) and Brent ran $61.98 → $138.21 → $125.44** ([Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/publications/the-plunging-price-of-thought); [TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20260331-12995.html); [Eurostat](https://www.formatresearch.com/en/2026/10/02/linflazione-annua-nellarea-euro-fino-al-38-eurostat/); [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=DCOILBRENTEU)).

## bull

- **Earnings, not hope, carry the index.** Forward P/E 20.5× (S&P 500) and 23× (tech) against 25× and 55× in early 2000, with the strongest earnings growth since 2021 and the top 10 holding 42% of index value but 30% of earnings — a premium, not a 2000-style mania ([Montgomery](https://www.montinvest.com/investor-insights/insights/nothing-to-see-here); [AInvest](https://www.ainvest.com/news/ten-stocks-70-index-work-number-timestamp-2610/)).
- **The spend is increasingly customer-funded.** Oracle's RPO reached $664bn with $30bn of new AI bookings "without requiring additional capital from Oracle"; Microsoft remains FCF-positive with backlog +84%; Oracle GPUs ran at 97.9% utilisation and four-year-old capacity renewed at a 20% premium ([Oracle Q1 FY27](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/09/11/oracle-orcl-q1-2027-earnings-call-transcript/); [TMT Finance](https://www.tmtfinance.com/intel/2026-hyperscaler-capex-tops-us700bn-analysis)).
- **Deflation is the diffusion mechanism.** A 13×/year fall in the price of a fixed capability is how AI reaches the whole economy; JPMorgan expects the payoff to "eventually show up as productivity as diffusion deepens" ([Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/publications/the-plunging-price-of-thought); [JPM AM](https://am.jpmorgan.com/us/en/asset-management/liq/insights/market-insights/market-updates/on-the-minds-of-investors/how-much-of-the-ai-boom-is-adding-to-us-growth/)).

## bear

- **The financing structure is the weak point, and it is repricing.** Hyperscaler debt $25bn/yr (2021–24) → $109bn (2025) → $218bn in ten months of 2026, ~18% of US IG supply; Oracle CDS at a record 261bp with >20% market-implied five-year default probability, even Nvidia >7%; a single-tenant AI project bond cleared at 9.25% ([CRISIL](https://integraliq.crisil.com/en/homepage/what-we-think/all-our-thinking/blog/2026/10/the-ai-debt-supercycle.html); [Bloomberg](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/ai-debt-spree-hammers-tech-222451927.html); [BigGo](https://finance.biggo.com/news/c6839228-1358-48aa-8a9b-b52ccdc4c60f)).
- **Hyperscaler free cash flow has already broken.** Alphabet's Q2 FCF was −$5.9bn, its first negative since the 2004 IPO, with long-term debt $98.2bn versus ~$16bn a year earlier; Amazon's TTM FCF was −$7.6bn; Meta's was $784m with buybacks stopped and $24.9bn of new debt. Capex is now capital-markets-funded, and that is what makes it cyclical ([Yahoo/GuruFocus](https://sg.finance.yahoo.com/news/alphabet-lifts-capex-205-billion-123356486.html); [TMT Finance](https://www.tmtfinance.com/intel/2026-hyperscaler-capex-tops-us700bn-analysis)).
- **Every major official warned in 2026, in plumbing-level terms.** BIS: "risks of the same asset being pledged multiple times," with credit repricing "similarly disruptive" to 2008; IMF: excess leverage "when monetization is uncertain"; BoE's FPC: debt and high leverage mean a sentiment shift "could amplify losses across global equities and credit" ([BIS](https://www.bis.org/publ/arpdf/ar2026e.htm); [IMF](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202610/13/WS6ace24c0e4b06d4aa0562969.html); [BoE](https://www.thenews.com.pk/amp/1418369-ai-boom-could-trigger-severe-market-shocks-bank-of-england-warns)).
- **Concentration is past 2000 and breadth is gone.** Top-10 ≈41% of the index versus ~27% at the 2000 peak, one JPMorgan basket at 49%, ten stocks delivering ~70% of 2026's gain while 83% of members sit more than 10% below their highs — a leverage-and-flow risk rather than a pure multiple risk ([AInvest](https://www.ainvest.com/news/ten-stocks-70-index-work-number-timestamp-2610/); [Seeking Alpha](https://in.tradingview.com/news/seekingalpha:181036896094b:0-jpmorgan-s-ai-basket-represents-49-of-the-s-p-500/)).
- **The measured payoff is small and being eaten by prices.** BIS ~1pp, JPMorgan 0.47pp, Goldman ISG ≈0.1pp, Acemoglu ≈1.5% of GDP over ten years and ≈5% of tasks; meanwhile computer-investment prices rose 10.9% y/y and ~1/6 of nominal tech-investment growth was price ([BIS](https://www.bis.org/publ/arpdf/ar2026e.htm); [JPM AM](https://am.jpmorgan.com/us/en/asset-management/liq/insights/market-insights/market-updates/on-the-minds-of-investors/how-much-of-the-ai-boom-is-adding-to-us-growth/); [Acemoglu](https://enterpriseai.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/industry/nobel-laureate-daron-acemoglu-prediction-on-ai-here-is-what-he-says/134768566)).
- **The trade is leveraged and forced selling is live.** Oracle trades ≈60% below its high while RPO grows, and Citadel unwound >80% of Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness portfolio risk (>$4bn) after the fund sold its entire public equity book under liquidity pressure ([Investing.com](https://ca.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/citadel-unwinds-80-of-aschenbrenners-portfolio-risk--report-4812510)).

### the tell to watch

- **Bear tell (credit leads equity):** Oracle's CDS beyond 261bp, any single-tenant AI project bond printing above ~9.25%, or a failed AI bond/SPV. A hyperscaler capex-guide cut would follow the credit move, not lead it.
- **Bull tell (breadth, not price):** equal-weight RSP closing its ≈4.5% gap to the cap-weighted index while RPO (~$664bn) and GPU utilisation (97.9%) hold.
