The Mirror

A sovereign knowledge repository. A lattice for AI to walk. A map for territory you already own.

What this is: The public architecture of Uqbar — a personal LLM wiki that compounds across sessions, where AI reads your material, synthesizes it, cross-links it, and eventually learns to speak in your voice. This repo contains the method, the concepts, and the proof that it works.

What this is not: A tutorial. A course. Generic advice. This is a worked example — a building you can walk through, see how it's constructed, and adapt to your own material.

Who This Is For

You have material. Years of notes. A blog. A podcast. Work logs. A book manuscript. Training journals. Client case studies. You sense that AI could amplify what's in there, but you don't know how. You've tried ChatGPT and it gives you generic answers because it doesn't know your material.

This repo shows you what's possible when AI has persistent access to your lived thought.

What's Inside

  • schema.md — The constitution. How the wiki is structured. Rules for ingestion, linking, maintenance.
  • wiki/ — The living synthesis. Concepts, technical patterns, method guides.
  • wiki/knowledge/ — Concept pages (void, compression, recursion, forgetting, creatures...)
  • wiki/technical/ — Engineering patterns from real ML production systems
  • wiki/personal/soul-profile.md — How the AI learned a voice (abstracted, no private details)
  • wiki/method/ — How to build your own, the splinter technique, podcast as tour guide
  • wiki/resources/ — Podcast and blog indices
  • raw/ — Immutable source material (currently empty; in the private Uqbar this holds transcripts, exports, archives)

How to Use This

As a reader: Start with wiki/index.md. Walk through the concepts. Notice how every page links to others. Notice the splinters at the bottom of key pages — places where the structure admits its own limitations. By the end, you should understand the pattern, not just the content.

As a builder: Read wiki/method/how-to-build.md. Then read the schema. Then look at how the concept pages are structured — frontmatter, cross-links, splinters. Then gather your own material and start.

With AI: Clone this repo. Point Hermes (or any AI agent) at it. Say: "Read the schema. Read the concepts. Now help me build my own." The AI will use this as a map for constructing your territory.

The Moat

The llm-wiki technique is public. Karpathy's gist has thousands of stars. The skill is open source.

The moat is not the method. The moat is the material. What you've lived, thought, written, made, loved, failed at. That cannot be downloaded. That cannot be replicated. That is what makes your wiki sovereign.

Origin

This is the outward breath of Uqbar — a private wiki built from 16 years of writing, a 57-post blog, 16 podcast episodes, work archives, love letters, and a near-death threshold. That wiki runs on Hermes, a persistent AI agent that maintains it across sessions. It has produced a letter that reached a real person, diagnoses that wounded its own author, and concepts that the author had lived but never named.

The private Uqbar remains private. This repo is the method extracted — stripped of everything that belongs to specific people, but keeping everything that might serve as a blueprint for your own sovereign intelligence.
