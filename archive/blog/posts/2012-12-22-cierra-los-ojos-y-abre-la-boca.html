Cierra los ojos y abre la boca

Entra un ciego en una reunión de lobotomizados y dice:

"Shi todosh losh eshpañoles trabajaran con el empeño de losh militaresh, EshpÁña iría mejor"

En esto que sale un español jugando a ser inglés y dice:

"Mire usté, ésta es mi carta de presentación:

As I am performing a proactive job searching, and your company is effectively looking to fulfill a position, I have addressed to you.

I am a young engineer experienced in tunneling construction through drill and blast procedure and as Maths and Science tutor. That is cause and effect at the same time for the responsibility and solvency I can bring to any meaningful task in which I am required to. Working everyday with tens of thousands euros, explosives inside the bowels of mountains, twenty teenagers and some doubtful university students willing to understand equations and natural laws which are going to bring them to their dreamed professions, students that refuse to be it, or rude mining workers thirty years experienced; all that represents a challenge pushes me to take something in my hands and do my best for it. Just the same as when I train boxing, when I hike to the tops of Spanish mountains.

Now I am searching for something new to fight for, and I am confident here in London there are things wanted to be enhanced, services to be offer, responsibilities to be taken. If anything has to be moved in a certain direction, I give my full commitment for it.

I attach here my CV, wishing to continue in contact with you.

Thank you very much for your attention.

London, December 2012."

Con lo que el ciego le salta:

"Ai don nou!"

A que es bueno!!!
