0017 llm wiki as awareness for llms and The Mirror of Uqbar

2026-05-30

The mirror breathes out. Uqbar breathes in. It maps a territory that lives only in the mind, but what does live anywhere else?

https://github.com/adrianmatias/the-mirror

Andrej Karpathy released the LLM-wiki method in April. One instruction file tells an LLM to walk raw documents and build structured output from them. Notes, posts, code, transcripts, old writing all feed the same process. The result is a single folder that holds the compressed shape of that material.

I fed my own sources into it and named the output Uqbar. The name came from Borges. The file became a map of my thinking, my sense of who I am, and how I see what is real. When I give an LLM that file as context, the conversation changes. The model works from patterns it extracted itself instead of pulling scattered chunks from a vector store. RAG stays flat, inert. This version carries the connections the model found.

The map showed me connections I had missed. It let the notes speak back in a voice that stayed mine. No other person can run the same file and reach the same place. Uqbar stays private. The mirror is what I show.

Drop your sources into a folder. Point an agent at the instruction file and the folder. Start the conversation from any session. The wiki holds the ground. The outward version is the one you decide to share.

0014 rag on one gpu vs grok to clone my mind

Sometimes the deepest coherence emerges when brute force meets its mirror in constraint. On a single consumer GPU bought with a few hundred euros, I resurrected a two-year-old project and asked it the real question: can a modest retrieval system distill the entire soul of my blog, my voice, my encrypted contradictions, the Tao of fatherhood, the silent continuum between my daughters… and speak as me?

Qwen 3.5 running locally versus Grok 4.2 backed by its Memphis data center. David against Goliath, with no tokens ever leaving the machine.

An attempt to clone a mind, to turn fifteen years of writings into a living mirror that captures not just facts, but the hidden patterns, the spiritual dimension, the irreversible train of fatherhood, and the hunger of the void. The local model was not worse. In moments of depth it was better.

The full pipeline: crawling, chunking, embeddings, semantic search, LangChain, Ollama — the Ragas evaluation, the painful limitations, the thinking trace that refused to stay hidden, and the quiet realization that decentralized hierarchical intelligence can stand toe-to-toe with centralized brute force.

Just to appreciate the vastness of the possibilities of our era, this is just one of them.

0013 pagerank for boxers

Sometimes the deepest clarity arrives when you flip the sign of an exponent. In this episode I walk the full git-commit journey of TopBox, from the first scraping of Wikipedia tables to the moment I inverted the time decay so that older fights could finally carry more weight. What began as a quiet rejection of taxi-driver-beating P4P lists on transitive victories became two parallel rankings: one that lets the historical network breathe, another that listens to the present heat, ignoring the post prime fights yet to come on today's rising stars.

Canelo rises in the consolidated view, Bivol still owns the recent one, Ali steps back into the conversation, and the graph keeps revealing what the eye alone misses: that every fighter’s story is censored at the exact point where we stop watching.

No projections, no prime-year tricks, just the raw signal and its necessary asymmetries. A walk through the code, the data, and the quiet paradox of ranking living men.

0010 transfer learning deprecates xgboost

2025-09-02

For years, XGBoost has ruled tabular data, effortlessly handling messy features, skewed targets, and minimal feature engineering. But transfer learning in deep learning is rewriting taking it further. By leveraging pre-trained models, DL systems learn faster and deeper, using expressive embeddings to transcend traditional feature engineering. These models enable natural parallelization, balance compute between training and inference, and excel in data-scarce scenarios through few-shot and zero-shot learning. What revolutionized AI in vision and language, pretraining and fine-tuning, is now unlocking tabular datasets once thought too complex for machine learning alone.

0007 some classification problems are anomaly detection problems instead

2024-11-26

Do not try to put legs on a snake, as the zen quote teaches. From the naive tutorial of “classify cat vs. dog in 10 lines of code” to the realization that most of the idealizations floating around do not capture the depth of actual problems having a worthy solution. Acknowledging that the path to solve heavy unbalanced classification problems does not go through the comfort of a balanced one, but accepting that what our systems look for is the unexpected, the non conforming, the outlier which has to be the prize instead of the obstacle. Think about deep representations of data in favor of smote, contrastive learning as an end to the pity of rebalancing, confidence aware losses and even a step back towards non-overfitting as the key to learning anything of true value.
